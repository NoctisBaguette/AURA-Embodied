# AURA-Embodied Research Roadmap

## Current Identity

AURA-Embodied studies adaptive robotic manipulation intelligence for generalizable physical agents.

The project focuses on the intelligence system enabling robots to understand, act, adapt, recover, and improve.

---

## Research Areas

### Existing Systems

Understand current approaches:

- VLM
- VLA
- world models
- memory systems
- planners
- policies

### Manipulation Intelligence

Study:

- grasping
- placing
- transporting
- pushing/pulling
- inserting
- tool use
- assembly

### Long Horizon Intelligence

Study:

- task decomposition
- memory
- verification
- failure recovery
- experience-based improvement

### Embodied AI Systems

Study:

- perception
- reasoning
- action
- control
- deployment

---

## Research Loop

```
Study
 ↓
Reproduce
 ↓
Experiment
 ↓
Analyze failure
 ↓
Improve
 ↓
Create new hypotheses
```

---

## Important Principle

AURA-Embodied does not wait for a final architecture before experimenting.

Research progress comes from building, testing, measuring, and improving systems.
