1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

We are entering the LingBot-VLA 2.0 competition's RoboTwin simulation track. A policy takes three camera images, the robot's joint/gripper state and a task instruction, then predicts robot actions. Our training uses the prescribed clean demonstrations across 50 Aloha-AgileX tasks. The submitted model must be accompanied by working code, evaluation results and reproduction instructions.

The official evaluation scope is 50 tasks × 2 settings (clean and randomized) × 100 episodes = 10,000 episodes. Our current 32-episode baseline and 32-episode candidate comparison are a small pilot, not that complete evaluation.

We now have three concurrent jobs:
- Captain Baguette + the existing assistant: operate the current run, review evidence, choose a checkpoint and integrate the submission.
- A: make the model exportable, independently loadable and reproducible.
- B: build full-suite evaluation, resumption and correct result accounting.

These are substantial engineering assignments. They do not require designing another model or starting another training experiment.
