# AURA Frontier Watch — 2026-10-15

**Coverage:** October 8–14, 2026 (as of Thursday in Asia/Shanghai). **Research status:** author-reported results; no independent AURA replication. This dated issue is complemented by three detailed files: [new papers](2026-10-15-new-papers.md), [cross-check and interpretation](2026-10-15-cross-check.md), and [02/06 experiments](2026-10-15-experiments.md).

## Executive signal

The most consequential new evidence concerns **reusable validated skills, cost-aware intervention, revisable task beliefs, and selective reasoning**. This week further narrows AETHER's easy novelty claims. It is not enough to assemble a VLA + planner + memory + verifier + recovery. Several external systems now do substantial parts of that.

**Working hypothesis, refined:** Reliable manipulation requires task-relevant evidence and history, beliefs that can be revised when physical facts change, action-relevant predictions when helpful, and recovery/learning mechanisms chosen with actual intervention and latency costs. Whether the implementation is explicit state, learned history, code, compiled skills, or a hybrid remains an experimental question.

## Embodied-AI upstream check

Read the newest report: https://github.com/NoctisBaguette/Embodied-AI-Research/blob/main/reports/frontier_watch/2026/2026-10-13.md as well as upstream CURRENT.md and TREND_LEDGER.md. **Upstream discoveries:** RoboJEPA, Long-WAM, REMM, RESCUE, MeMoREval, Spa-Bench, GAMMA, Grounded-Dreamer, Fly-by-Code; plus Ultra/Physical Intelligence deployment coverage. AURA confirms and elevates **REMM** (evidence-backed revisable task state) and **RESCUE** (the decision value of an alarm/intervention, not mere detection accuracy). RoboJEPA is important but does not imply infinite planning horizons.

**Independent AURA additions, not emphasized upstream:** RoboRSI, SEAR, EmbodiedEvo, ForkCast, LoopWAM, ChunkAct, WorldOracle, ForceWAM, LIBERO-RIG, Chronos Policy, SpatialHarness, DreamTrue, Dream to Remember, AXIS-Bench, EqC, ACCRUE. Most new October 14 listings were submitted October 13.

**Interpretation difference:** Explicit task state can be useful (REMM) without demanding a separate external-memory module for everything (Long-WAM and ChunkAct demonstrate integrated/compiled alternatives). Recovery action must be selected for value (RESCUE/ForkCast), not automatically triggered by every alarm. Claims of experience-driven learning require held-out transfer and matched practice budgets (SEAR).

## AURA decisions

**DEEP DIVE:** RoboRSI, SEAR, ForkCast, RESCUE, REMM, LoopWAM. **READ → DEEP DIVE:** ChunkAct, EmbodiedEvo, WorldOracle, ForceWAM, LIBERO-RIG, RoboJEPA. **READ/WATCH:** Chronos Policy, MeMoREval, Spa-Bench, SpatialHarness, DreamTrue, Dream to Remember, EqC, ACCRUE, AXIS-Bench, BT-Agent; other evidence-driven contact/robustness benchmarks.

**REPRODUCE priority:** (1) small revisable task-state graph, (2) counterfactual cost-aware intervention at saved simulator checkpoints, (3) per-primitive vs event/boundary-triggered planning, (4) skill-local correction plus held-out transfer, (5) setup perturbation and physical-prediction fidelity subset.
