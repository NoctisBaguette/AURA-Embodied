## 2. High-impact independent findings (all metrics below are AUTHOR-REPORTED)

### A. RoboDoctor — training-free execution-time recursive revision

**What happened?** [RoboDoctor, arXiv:2610.35604 v1, Oct 26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35604) develops a training-free framework with policy/agent allocation, selective intervention, grounded physical correction, and persistent reason→method→outcome→reuse records. The [official THUSI-Lab/RoboDoctor repository](https://github.com/THUSI-Lab/RoboDoctor) was inspected (README blob 14563a24cab968a718d0cff785883da40cd8e994): public controllers, tests, dependencies and scripts exist; code license/third-party/model terms require individual checks. In a curated **54-task** RoboDojo-Sim set the authors report mean five-dimension **57.10**, native success **49.44%**, and a refinement improvement from **49.74 to 57.10** score. The README reports **162/162** valid warmup and **301/324** valid refinement episodes: invalid/excluded attempts matter.

**Why/capability?** Its explicit link from execution diagnosis to correction and experience is near-direct prior art for the AURA recovery/learning hypothesis, not a generic VLA leaderboard entry. **AURA care: DEEP DIVE — MONITOR/BACKLOG, not current build.** **Possible reproduction/improvement:** WP1/Core can compare same-policy/same-task gating and action-library baselines, intervention cost, excluded attempts and held-out reuse; the authors explicitly do **not** isolate cross-episode experience causally. Full RoboDojo/Isaac Sim 5.1 + policy/model assets and model-service dependencies are unsuitable as a quick M9a addition. Public code ≠ installed stack ≠ audited reproduction.

### B. RoboAuto — active physical grounding and program search

**What happened?** [RoboAuto, arXiv:2610.36533 v1, Oct 27](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36533) proposes object-, scene- and interaction-level knowledge, current-scene grounding, staged robot program search, self-designed probes of uncertain hypotheses, and checks of actual expected physical effects. Its [project page](https://roboauto-ai.github.io/) describes the system; code availability was not independently established as runnable at scan time. Authors claim **89.1%** short LIBERO-PRO task success and gains on longer and real-robot tasks under their conditions.

**Why/capability?** Object-centric affordance/state semantics and *active information acquisition* are exactly within AURA's long-term direction. This is strong prior art against novelty claims based on a knowledge base plus verifier. **AURA care: DEEP DIVE — MONITOR/BACKLOG.** **Possible reproduction/improvement:** analyze where it obtains ground truth and calibration, how it distinguishes uncertainty from failure, and whether an active test improves a held-out manipulation task compared with identical passive sensing. Future tests should count extra observations/moves and forbid evaluator leakage; M9a is not the place to add the probing actuator.

### C. PDRVLA — world prediction may be useful without online world-model generation

**What happened?** [PDRVLA, arXiv:2610.37381 v1, Oct 27](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37381) trains lightweight physical-dynamics tokens to predict future RGB/depth/flow features using auxiliary supervision from a frozen video world model. At inference the policy conditions action on those physical tokens instead of generating videos. Authors report **LIBERO 99.3%, LIBERO-Plus 89.9%, RoboTwin 2.0 93.6%, SimplerEnv 81.5%** under heterogeneous task settings; no AURA measurement.

**Why/capability?** **A direct challenge** to treating an online bounded world model as necessary: predictive competence may be **distilled into a fast actor**. **AURA care: DEEP DIVE concept; NEXT-CHECKPOINT comparison option only if a compatible learned baseline exists** (not an additional approved experiment). **Possible reproduction/improvement:** compare action-only, supervised physical tokens and runtime predictor at matched policy, training data and actual latency on unseen physical changes. Demand grounding in observed effects, not just visual feature loss. No runnable checkpoint confirmed in this pass.

### D. Rollback — simulated counterfactual backtracking for policy *learning*

**What happened?** [Rollback, arXiv:2610.36068 v1, Oct 26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36068) rewinds decision points **inside a learned world model** to branch alternative actions, distill action-level supervision, and update the model from real transitions; authors report absolute +10.67/+11.11/+11.85 percentage points across three real tasks.

**Why/capability?** A concrete closed learning loop from counterfactual outcomes; **not the same as physically rewinding the robot** and not proof of safe on-policy recovery. **AURA care: READ→DEEP DIVE, MONITOR/BACKLOG.** **Possible reproduction/improvement:** first test checkpoint-branched *real simulator* oracles without a large WAM, enforcing restoration of physics/controller/policy/RNG state and controlling branching budgets. Then ask if a learned predictor's ranking is calibrated and robust under domain shift.

### E. E0.5 — make future 3D geometry the model backbone

**What happened?** [E0.5, arXiv:2610.36011 v1, Oct 26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36011) uses a 3D foundation as the primary WAM backbone, predicts future 3D geometry alongside actions; authors report RoboTwin 2.0 **89.90% clean versus 50.09% randomized** without embodied pretraining, then real-robot improvements using 160k+ AgiBotWorld-Beta trajectories. **Why/capability?** Geometric physical prediction rather than only visual-plausible video. **AURA care: READ, WATCH future model baseline**. **Possible reproduction/improvement:** benchmark pose/contact effects under randomized calibration and object shifts on matched tasks; the large clean/random gap cautions against declaring generalization. Not a 3D backbone migration for M9a.

### F. ActiveAlign and VLA-Stem — sensing and fast corrective control

**What happened?** [ActiveAlign, arXiv:2610.36859 v1, Oct 27](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36859) uses purposeful robot motion plus differentiable render alignment for hand-eye calibration, and physical touch to refine object alignment. [VLA-Stem, arXiv:2610.37378 v1, Oct 27](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37378) adds a fast geometric/safety motion-refinement layer under a frozen VLA. **Why/capability?** Active perception can remove sensing error before planning; reflex control can preserve safety and timing during larger policy chunks, but neither guarantees an intended placement or insertion was achieved.

**AURA care:** ActiveAlign **READ — future WP2/09 bridge**; VLA-Stem **READ/WATCH — later learned-policy integration**. **Possible reproduction/improvement:** after M9a neutral cameras work, quantify calibration drift/uncertainty with existing sensors, no new touching hardware. Separately, compare fast correction with and without a task-level verifier on matched perturbations; avoid conflating collision avoidance with task success.

### G. UniManip and RoboDojo — prior art and external evaluation controls

**What happened?** [UniManip, arXiv:2602.13086, v1 Feb 13 / revision Oct 24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13086), combines high-level orchestration, scene-level object-centric graph, geometric action grounding and memory-guided recovery. It is a **revised older work**, not a newly launched October system. Official [RoboDojo news/leaderboard](https://robodojo-benchmark.com/) records **GO-3 Nano Preview** on Oct 27 at **61.90 five-dimension score / 56.42% sim agent-track success**, PyRUA-Lean Oct 24 at **55.26 / 50.35%**, plus a separate real track on Oct 26. These are posted benchmark entries, not our native results.

**Why/capability?** Generic memory+agent+recovery architecture is heavily occupied, and aggregate leaderboard rankings mix different policies, reviewers, priors and costs. **AURA care: DEEP DIVE UniManip prior art; WATCH leaderboard**. **Possible reproduction/improvement:** maintain the competitor matrix with same executor, same intervention and budget where possible; report agent/policy categories and separate Gen/Precision/Horizon/Memory/Open dimensions. Do not call a category-first system universally superior.

## 3. Upstream confirmed by AURA — limits and different interpretations

**AW-0** [arXiv:2610.30136, Oct 21](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30136) jointly models executable-*looking* skill code, future video and action; inspected [official repo](https://github.com/ZhuoyangLiu2005/agentic-wam), README blob 2a576f04955448afa6c62d879e01dbf0a9060bf6. The author README specifies the **default model-generated program is conditioning, not code executed by the robot**. Checkpoints and training code exist but complete pretraining corpora/starting video model weights are not bundled. **READ** as code/world/action alternative; no full reproduction.

**P² Progress Probes** [arXiv:2610.31613, Oct 22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31613): frozen-policy activation probe predicts progress; **Echo** [arXiv:2610.31569, Oct 22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31569): stop/failure jointly trained with action. Both compete with large external verifiers, **but progress or stop prediction ≠ verified physical effect and alarm ≠ worthwhile intervention**. They require a learned baseline; M9a remains a sensor-boundary study. **DEEP DIVE on measurement/ablation, not immediate integration**.

**FastHarness** [arXiv:2610.31427, Oct 22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31427) inspected [official repo](https://github.com/FastHarness/fast-harness), README blob 0f2a847b21517eee010f4b3dd8c8f1da9b0b55cf. A reproducible **CPU-only symbolic demo** is documented; live RoboDojo depends on separately provided policy/simulator/reviewer servers. **REPRODUCE candidate for Embodied-AI's experiment owner only** if HQ selects it; AURA should reuse the result, not duplicate it. Treat reduced calls as meaningful only when missed failures and final success are counted.

**WholeBodyMem/HuMeGo**, UniWALM, UniFlowWAM, OpenTac, Isaac Lab 3.0 and NVIDIA Oct 20–21 announcements remain **upstream-identified context**. Independent novelty claims for this week are not inferred from those older items. High memory performance author figures do not validate a generally downloadable HuMeGo corpus. New platform releases are not reason to replace frozen ManiSkill or buy sensors.

## 4. External comparison, AURA hypothesis update and risk

| External system | Overlap with AURA's hypothesis | Specific remaining question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DynaHarness (earlier)** | Execution governance, failure attribution, changing capabilities/replanning | Does governance help with **the same** capability library and policy under matched costs and nonprivileged observations? |
| **RoboDoctor (Oct 26)** | Selective policy/agent switching, corrections and persistent experience | What is the **isolated held-out** benefit of experience versus additional retries/coverage? |
| **RoboAuto (Oct 27)** | Object-centric scene/effect models, active probing, staged verification | Does active information gathering beat passive sensing **after** controlling observation/motion cost? |
| **UniManip (Feb; updated Oct 24)** | Hierarchical scene/operation graph, geometric grounding, diagnosis/recovery | Which state semantics remain reliable when camera calibration, object occlusion or geometry is uncertain? |
| **PDRVLA (Oct 27)** | Action-relevant physical futures under real-time constraints | Can predictive competence be trained into the actor, eliminating an online WAM for the same success/latency? |

**Interpretation:** AURA's unique contribution cannot be “policy + planner + memory + verifier + recovery” or simply “recursive self improvement”. The defensible scientific opportunity is evidence of *which* mechanisms are necessary under **non-privileged sensing**, changed physical state, matched intervention costs and genuine transfer, and when simpler or integrated architectures suffice. Prior art constrains novelty, **not** the value of AURA's already-audited instrument.

**Persistent hypothesis refinement:** action-relevant prediction can occur **during training** and be distilled into fast policy representations (PDRVLA), so a **separate online world model is optional** until demonstrated valuable. Retain revisable state and cost-aware intervention as *testable alternatives*, not mandatory modules. The weekly discoveries do not invalidate M9a.
